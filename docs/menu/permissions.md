# Permissions

Owners only.

- **Groups** shows each group's parents, protection level, allowed and denied
  permissions and members, with controls to change them, create groups and
  delete them.
- **Players** shows players with explicit groups or permissions.
- **Check access** explains whether a player has a permission, and why.

Every change runs the matching owner-only policy command, asks for confirmation
first, and is versioned and audited. See
[permissions](../reference/permissions.md) for how a decision is resolved.
