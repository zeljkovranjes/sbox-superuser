# Bans & mutes

Lists every active ban and text mute you can see, across all players, newest
first. Filter by Steam ID.

**Expires** shows the time left, such as `in 2h 14m`; hover it for the exact UTC
time. **Unban** and **Unmute** ask for a reason and a confirmation before they
run. Click a row to open that player.

Bans are visible with `admin.players.ban`, `admin.players.unban` or
`admin.players.history`; text mutes with `admin.players.mute`,
`admin.players.unmute` or history.
